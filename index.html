About the Fest
What is Karma '26?

Karma — the crowning jewel of KMCT College of Engineering's annual calendar — stands as the epitome of innovation, collaboration, and cultural richness. This Annual National Level Techno-Socio-Cultural Extravaganza is a grand celebration that goes far beyond the ordinary.

At Karma, we create an immersive experience that seamlessly integrates technology, socio-cultural vibrancy, and academic excellence. Join us in this grand celebration, where each moment is a testament to the limitless potential within the KMCT College of Engineering community.
